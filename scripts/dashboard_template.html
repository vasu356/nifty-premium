    Method and assumptions

    • Data: NSE F&O bhavcopies (end-of-day close per contract) and NSE daily index closes. One missing bhavcopy (30 Mar 2021) was filled from NSE's market-activity report.
    • Entry: at the day's closing price, nearest weekly expiry, out-of-the-money strikes that traded at least one contract that day. Strike chosen with the close nearest the premium target inside the band.
    • Exit: next trading day's close. If the next day is expiry, the option settles at intrinsic value against the Nifty close (no closing order, no slippage).
    • Costs per leg: 0.5 pt slippage per order, ₹20 brokerage per order, STT on sell premium (0.0625% before Oct 2024, 0.1% after; 0.125% on exercised long options), exchange charges (0.05% / 0.0353%), SEBI fee, stamp duty 0.003% on buys, 18% GST on brokerage and charges. Brokerage is divided by the lot size in force on that day ().
    • Stops: checked against the next day's high. If the day opened above the stop, the fill is the open price. The "exact fill" variant assumes a perfect fill at the stop and is shown only to illustrate how much gaps cost.
    • Not modelled: intraday entry/exit timing, bid-ask spread beyond the slippage assumption, margin and its funding cost, taxes on profits. Closing prices for thinly traded strikes can be stale.
